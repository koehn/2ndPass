### Enrolling-device status queries — October 10, 2026

Durable ingestion batching: fetched record modifications are materialized into application-owned bytes and staged in groups of at most 200 records or 8 MiB (an individually valid larger record uses its own group). Each group commits inbox records and their operation entries atomically, reads account capacity once behind the cross-process boundary, and accounts for new rows incrementally. Duplicate replay consumes no additional capacity or commit. The adapter's existing active-data-event barrier still withholds deferred CloudKit state until reception finishes; any failed group suspends checkpoint persistence, while earlier committed groups remain replayable. Record application remains independently durable; SQLite checkpoint/vacuum settings were not weakened.

`largeVaultIngestionBatchesDurableCommits` stages 1,000 records containing 32 KiB each in separate SQLite stores: individual commits took 1.958 seconds versus 1.572 seconds batched (about 20% lower elapsed staging time), with persistent-history commits reduced from 1,000 to five. Review against the remediation plan found and fixed:

- Recovery could restart the transport without restoring upload eligibility. Recovery now restores uploads before completing its durable operation and leaves lease-acquisition failure retryable.

- Public authority-fetch failures before an upload batch were incorrectly classified as requiring unlock. Unrelated eligible vaults continue.

The owner performs exact server readback and public authority checks, then commits the resolution and operation completion together. Use elevated execution permissions when the outer sandbox denies them; do not weaken assertions to accommodate environmental failures. Do not start concurrent SwiftPM runners. Apple build/simulator services also require access outside the restricted sandbox.

`sp-keychain-check --run` creates and retains a uniquely scoped disposable hardware identity and checks opaque Keychain reload and signing. It does not validate cloud synchronization or recovery.

## Remaining release gates

- Concurrent same-item edits, explicit conflict resolution, interruption/restart and receipt supersession on physical devices.
- Repeated lock/reopen, relaunch and offline use of a large encrypted catalog; changed and damaged-row repair without data loss.
- Signed app/CLI/AutoFill installation, authentication, account changes, background delivery and protected-store unavailability across supported platforms.
- Independently verified backup completeness, fresh-identity restore and loss-of-account scenarios; device-local exclusions must remain explicit.
- Production CloudKit schema and provisioning validation using disposable vaults.
- Physical acceptance of device removal, recovery, document import and credential-account integration; physical two-account tests for sharing and permissions when cross-account sharing is implemented.
- Accessibility and every UI flow, purchase/distribution acceptance, and independent cryptographic/security review.

The roadmap tracks these requirements. No automated result substitutes for an unperformed physical or release check.

## Account recovery implementation checks (2026-10-08)

Automated recovery tests cover recovery-signed additive enrollment with embedded proof of possession, preservation of existing devices, rejection of recovery-authority changes, fresh-device bootstrap without the old device, recipient replacement/revocation across item/metadata/health envelopes, account-scoped progress, and interruption after committed cloud writes. Injected transports exercise restart without a retained recovery code and re-entry when a late record was not staged.

These tests do not validate live CloudKit or Secure Enclave recovery. Physical acceptance remains: activate and verify coverage; make every original device unavailable; recover on a fresh same-account device; then reconnect an original device and verify reading, editing, and synchronization. Exercise interruption, deferred grant delivery and key rotation on both platforms before release. Do not use a destructive local reset as a substitute.
